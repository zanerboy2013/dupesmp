Frequently Asked Questions (Q&A)

Click on any question below for detailed information.

How do I connect to DupeSMP? ▼
Open Minecraft Java or Bedrock edition, navigate to Multiplayer, click "Add Server", and enter IP: Dupesmp.faganscr.com and Port: 25565. Make sure you use an allowed client or version!
Are items allowed to be duplicated on DupeSMP? ▼
Yes! As the name suggests, DupeSMP embraces anarchy and item duplication mechanics, but you must still follow all server and chat rules regarding harassment, crashing the server, and illegal modifications.
How do I apply for staff or helper positions? ▼
Staff applications open periodically inside our official Discord server. Keep an eye on announcements and make sure you meet the age requirements and have a clean record before applying.
What should I do if I think I was falsely banned or blacklisted? ▼
If you believe your ban or blacklist was a mistake, you can open a ticket in our support Discord or reach out to an admin. Trolling staff in tickets will result in immediate denial of your appeal.
How does the Media Program payment work? ▼
Approved creators who sign our Media TOS receive compensation via in-store credit for DupeSMP, which can be redeemed for server store ranks, crates, keys, and perks. Check the Media TOS button above for full details.
